About the Photographer

African Photos and Videos curates high-quality photography and videography showcasing African people, places and stories.

Biography

Jane Doe is an award-winning African photographer whose work documents culture, city life, landscapes and human stories across the continent. Her images have been featured in editorial and commercial projects worldwide.

The photographer focuses on authentic representation and ethical storytelling — building relationships with communities and seeking permission where required for editorial use.

About the Photographer

African Photos and Videos curates high-quality photography and videography showcasing African people, places and stories.

Biography

Ariyo Olasunkanmi is a Nigerian photographer, videographer, drone pilot and visual storyteller whose work documents African culture, everyday life, business, technology, travel, landscapes and human experiences. Based in Lagos, Nigeria, he creates authentic visual content that reflects the diversity, energy and evolving stories of Africa.

With a strong background in graphic design, branding and visual communication, Ariyo combines creative direction with technical expertise to produce compelling imagery and video for editorial, commercial and digital media. His work has been licensed and featured through leading global stock-media platforms, reaching audiences and clients worldwide. His photography and videography focus on genuine representation, cultural relevance and thoughtful storytelling. He approaches each project with respect for people, communities and context, while maintaining ethical standards and obtaining appropriate permissions where required. Through photography, video and aerial imagery, Ariyo continues to build a diverse visual archive that helps brands, publishers and creative professionals discover high-quality African content.
